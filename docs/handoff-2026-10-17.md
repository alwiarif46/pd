### #36 CI failure (fixed in d1ab7ac, pushed)
- **What failed:** `invites.test.ts > a billing downgrade racing an accept` failed on CI (expected 403, got 200) but passed locally.
- **Code bug:** the accept route dated the membership with `clock_timestamp()`, which is wall-clock time in the session's time zone. The app stores UTC, and the local embedded Postgres runs in Asia/Calcutta, so members were dated 5.5 hours ahead. It now uses `clock_timestamp() at time zone 'UTC'`.
- **Test bug:** the bug above was hiding a test bug. The helper `addMember` used `Date.now()+1000`, so on CI (UTC) the invitee could sort before that member.
- **Fixes:**
  - The helper now uses the database clock in UTC.
  - The race test branches on the observed winner instead of assuming one.
  - The happy-path test checks that `createdAt` is within 60 s of now.
- **Verified:** 20 runs passed, in the local zone and with `TZ=UTC`. The full API suite also passes in both.
- **Still to do:** confirm CI is green on d1ab7ac.
- **Production:** make sure Postgres runs with `TimeZone=UTC`. Every other `created_at` default is `now()`, which is session-zone wall clock.
